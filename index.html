Carlos Trancoso (b. 1989) is a Portuguese visual artist based in Porto. Working across photography, installation and moving image, his practice explores the relationship between technology, ecology and contemporary image culture.

He is a member of the Barda collective and part of the FUTURES Photography platform through Ci.Clo. His work has been exhibited at festivals and institutions including Backlight Photo Festival, Photoalicante, Photo Open Up, Athens Digital Arts Festival, PHotoESPAÑA, Bienal de Fotografia do Porto and Encontros da Imagem.
